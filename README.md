### 🌟 Featured Projects

#### 1. [Alzheimer MRI Classification with DenseNet-169](https://github.com/Myon2000/Alzheimer-DenseNet-169)
> **Medical Imaging & Deep Learning Pipeline**  
> Deep Learning research utilizing the DenseNet-169 architecture with transfer learning to classify Alzheimer's disease progression stages from brain MRI scans. Dense inter-layer connectivity mitigates vanishing gradients while maximizing feature reuse.  
> `Python` `PyTorch` `DenseNet-169` `Medical AI` `Computer Vision`

#### 2. [NilaHealth: Aquaculture AI & Tilapia Disease Detection](https://github.com/Myon2000/NilaHealth2)
> **Smart Aquaculture Microservice Platform**  
> Developed in partnership with local tilapia fish farmers in Jember to reduce fish mortality. Couples a Laravel web portal with a Python Flask CNN microservice (EfficientNet) classifying 6 pathology states alongside automated curative SOP recommendations and pond maintenance scheduling.  
> `Laravel 12` `Python` `Flask` `CNN (EfficientNet)` `MySQL` `Microservices`

#### 3. [SiPuBi: Subsidized Fertilizer Distribution System](https://github.com/Myon2000/SiPuBi)
> **Certified Supply Chain Information System**  
> Enterprise system engineered to monitor and record subsidized fertilizer distribution workflows, ensuring transparent quota allocations and accountable delivery for registered farmer groups.  
> `PHP` `MySQL` `JavaScript` `Certified System` `Information System`

#### 4. [Integrated Dental Clinic Management System](https://github.com/Myon2000/web-klinik-gigi)
> **Healthcare Operational Platform (2026)**  
> Modern clinic operations application supporting conflict-free doctor consultation booking, periodic patient intake, and digital record-keeping for treatment procedures.  
> `JavaScript` `Full-Stack` `Clinic Operations` `Clean UI`

#### 5. [Employee Leave Management System](https://github.com/Myon2000/leave-management-system)
> **Enterprise HR Workflow & Role-Based Access**  
> Corporate leave automation portal with real-time balance calculations, audit-logged leave lifecycle tracking, and strict role separation between employees and managers.  
> `PHP` `MySQL` `RBAC` `Enterprise Workflow`

#### 6. [Lelangin & CekakArta: Financial Tools & Bidding Architecture](https://github.com/Myon2000/Lelangin)
> **Financial Computation & Online Auctions**  
> Explorations in digital bidding architectures with real-time price increments, transaction integrity validations, and structured personal financial calculations.  
> `Python` `JavaScript` `Financial Modeling` `Data Architecture`
